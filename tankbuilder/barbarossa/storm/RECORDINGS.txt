For use in the game, these recordings were excerpted, filtered, level matched
and resampled to 24 kHz mono PCM. Rain has a seamless loop crossfade. Thunder
excerpts have short end fades and play as separate one-shots after lightning.
The mix and the game do not imply endorsement by the recordists.
